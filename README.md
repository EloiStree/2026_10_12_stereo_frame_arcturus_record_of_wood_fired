# Wood-Fired Cooking Sample of QooCam

> Sampeling of QooCam photo


Photo are in DNG of 50 Mo produce by the camera without beeing edited.


I want to provide the community with photos and videos from the Arcturus Steam Frame video and QooCam Stereo Recorder, I recorded my father's wood-fired oven while helping my parents with the firewood.
Find more: [2026_10_12_stereo](https://github.com/EloiStree?tab=repositories&q=2026_10_12_stereo&type=&language=&sort=)
